# Third-party assets

- **Fonts** in `/fonts` are from Google Fonts and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (Barlow, Barlow Condensed, Oswald, Anton, Bebas Neue, Archivo Black, Inter, Playfair Display,
  DM Serif Display, Abril Fatface, Libre Caslon Text, Zilla Slab, Shrikhand, Bungee, Caveat).
- **Icons** come from Iconify packages: Phosphor (MIT), Fluent Emoji Flat (MIT),
  Game Icons (CC BY 3.0, credit game-icons.net when you use them).
- **Flags**: flag-icons (MIT).
- **Map data**: world-atlas and us-atlas (ISC), derived from Natural Earth and the U.S. Census Bureau.
- **Photos in the gallery** are fetched at render time from Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons and are
  not stored in this repo. They are under their own licenses (mostly CC BY-SA); credit them when
  you publish.

vivid-charts is an independent project. It is inspired by the editorial style of Visual Capitalist
and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. The example posters re-draw publicly reported data
to demonstrate chart forms.
